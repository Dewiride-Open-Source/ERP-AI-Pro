# ADR-0066: Document numbering: a series per kind of document, fixed for a year at its first number and taken in the issuing transaction

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-08

## Context

- **The roadmap item** `business-building-blocks-document-numbering-series` asks for configurable number series per kind of document and financial year, at most 16 characters, unique within a financial year, with prefix and reset rules, given without a gap even when documents are saved at the same time, proven by a parallel integration test. The sales invoices, credit and debit notes and vouchers of the finance phases number their documents with it.
- **The law (read 2026-11-08 on CBIC's consolidated CGST Rules and the GST Council's copies of the notifications).** Rules 46(b) (tax invoice), 49(b) (bill of supply), 50(b), 51(b) and 52(b) (receipt, refund and payment voucher) and 53(1)(d) (revised tax invoice), inserted by Notification No. 10/2017-Central Tax (G.S.R. 663(E), in force 1 July 2017), and 53(1A)(c) (credit or debit note), inserted by Notification No. 3/2019-Central Tax from 1 February 2019, each require "a consecutive serial number not exceeding sixteen characters, in one or multiple series, containing alphabets or numerals or special characters" "-" and "/", "unique for a financial year"; none was amended to 1 April 2026. The citation is rule 46(b): rule 46 has no sub-rules. Rule 54's documents and the delivery challan of rule 55(1), which names no characters, are not numbered yet. The Invoice Registration Portal refuses an e-invoice whose number begins with "0", "/" or "-" (Generate IRN API 1.03, validation 8) and, since 1 June 2025, compares numbers without regard to case.
- **A gap comes from numbering outside the document's transaction.** A sequence or a counter committed on its own keeps a number whose document then fails to save.
- **Owner decisions (2026-11-08).** Finance and administrators set up, change and see numbering, and nobody else sees it; every financial year starts again at 1; the menu entry "Document numbering" and the two permission sentences as written in the module's descriptor.

## Decision

- **A building block and a Platform module, as attachments and jobs are split.** `BuildingBlocks.Numbering` owns the schema `platform_numbering` (`NumberingDbContext`: `NumberSeries`, `NumberSeriesYears`), the catalogue of the kinds of document modules declare (`AddNumberedDocument(NumberedDocument(Key, Title, Description, SerialNumberRule))`, a key of three lower-case kebab segments), the numbers (`IDocumentNumbers`) and their administration (`INumberingAdministration`); `Platform/Numbering` (descriptor schema `null`, flag `Erp.Modules.Platform.Numbering`, permissions `platform.numbering.series.read` and `.change`, held by Finance) exposes `GET /documents`, `GET /series/{id}`, `POST /series` and `PUT /series/{id}` under `/api/platform/numbering` and implements `ISerialNumberRules`. A module numbers its documents through the building block, never through the module, whatever the module's flag says.
- **The number is taken in the issuing transaction.** A module's context maps the two tables (`public DbSet<NumberSeries> NumberSeries` and `public DbSet<NumberSeriesYear> NumberSeriesYears`), whose configurations, applied by `[EntityTypeConfiguration]`, leave them out of every migration but `NumberingDbContext`'s, as every context maps `audit.ChangeRecords`; the command that issues a document calls `NextAsync(context, documentType, documentDate)` last in its unit of work. It takes the application lock `platform_numbering.<key>` of the transaction (`TransactionLocks`, 10 seconds; busy answers 409 `concurrency.conflict`), reads the kind's numbering and the year of the document's date, opens that year if it is not open, gives its next number and moves it on; the year's row commits with the document or not at all, and the next save of that kind waits for the lock until then, so the numbers of a year run on without a gap or a repeat. A refusal is a `Result`: 422 `numbering.not-set-up`, `numbering.no-rule`, `numbering.format-not-allowed` (the numbering no longer meets a newly opened year's rule) and `numbering.exhausted`; asking outside a transaction, on a context that maps no numbering or for an undeclared kind throws. `PersistenceTests` admit the two types in any catalogue context, and `NumberingTests` hold every mapping to `platform_numbering.<table>`, left out of its migrations unless it is the owner's, over the product's contexts and three probe contexts.
- **A format, fixed for a year once the year gives a number.** `NumberFormat` (a complex type: prefix, the year shown as none, `2026-27` or `26-27`, a separator after it and the digits of the running number, which zeros fill and which grows past them) is the numbering's, and each year keeps its own copy with the most characters its rule allowed when it opened, its first and its next running number (`NumberSeriesYear`, unique per numbering and year). Setting a numbering up opens the current year at the next number asked, so a company moving from another system mid-year carries its numbers on; a change reformats the current year while it has given no number, and its next number may change only then (400 `numbering.year-in-use`); a year that has given one keeps its format, which becomes the next year's. Every financial year starts again at 1. One numbering per kind of document.
- **The rule is a statutory parameter, the start and the capitals the ERP's.** The new set `gst.document-serial-numbers` (basis law, as of `financialYearStart`, from 2026-04-01) holds by clause the most characters and the characters besides letters and numerals with the clause's words; a kind of document names its clause, and `GstSerialNumberRules` reads the set as of the year's first day, so a year's numbering is checked against the rule of its first day. A format is refused, keyed by its field, when a number would be longer than the rule allows, would contain a character it does not (the year's own "-" included), or would begin with "0", "/" or "-"; letters are capitals, so a number reads the same in the ERP as on the portal and in GSTR-1.
- **Records.** A numbering is recorded under `platform.numbering.series` and a year under `platform.numbering.series-years`, filed under its numbering; the running number is left out of the change records, so opening a year writes one record and each number given none, its document recording it; `NumberingRecordNames` names them by the kind's title and the year.

## Options rejected

- **A SQL Server sequence or a counter committed on its own**: a document that fails to save leaves a gap.
- **Optimistic concurrency on the year's row alone**: concurrent saves would fail and retry rather than wait their turn.
- **The numbering's tables in each issuing module's schema**: the numbering page could not show a year's numbers without reading every module, and its changes would not meet the numbers in the same rows.
- **A literal 16 in code**: the limit is statutory, so it is a parameter with its source.
- **Changing a year's format after it has given a number**: the year's numbers would no longer be one consecutive series.

## Consequences

- The issuing module's command takes the number after its other checks, because the lock is held until its document commits; its document keeps the text, the year and the running number, which a return's list of documents issued reads.
- No module declares a kind of document yet, so the page lists none in production until the sales invoices are built; the tests and the end-to-end host declare kinds of their own (`TestNumberedDocuments`), whose numbers the end-to-end host's test routes take and remove.
- A change of the law reaches the ERP as a new version of the set in a release; a year already open keeps the most characters it opened with.
- Rule 54's documents and the delivery challan are seeded with the items that number them.
