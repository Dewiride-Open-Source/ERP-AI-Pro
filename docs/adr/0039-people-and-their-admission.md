# ADR-0039: People and their admission

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-10

## Context

- Since ADR-0031 every person who completes a Microsoft Entra sign-in to the registration gets a session. The registration requires an app role assignment (`appRoleAssignmentRequired`, `scripts/azure/lib/graph.sh`), so only holders of `Erp.User` or `Erp.Admin` (`scripts/azure/entra/app-roles.json`) reach the ERP, but the ERP itself keeps no record of anybody: it cannot refuse one person, list who uses it, or hold what Entra does not (employee code, phone number, designation, date of joining).
- The session check (`SessionCookieEvents.ValidatePrincipal`) already reads three records on every request: the person's sign-out time (`SessionRevocations`, ADR-0032), the Entra session the cookie came from (`EntraSessions`, ADR-0036) and the person's token cache entry. Bearer tokens (ADR-0034) are off by default and no product route takes them yet.
- `BuildingBlocks.*` never reference a module (architecture test), and the authentication building block runs in the API host only.
- The scope of `user-management-users-module-and-just-in-time` names the schema `identity` and a first administrator bootstrapped from configuration. The naming rules give a module the schema `<domain>_<module>`, and the registration's app roles already say who administers.
- Owner decisions of 2026-10-10: every sub-phase of `user-management` is Community; until `user-management-roles-and-permissions`, holders of the app role `Erp.Admin` manage people, with no configuration bootstrap; administrators can also register a person before their first sign-in and delete a record, and the designation is free text until `user-management-departments-designations-and-reporting-lines`; deactivating a person ends their open sessions at their next request, not only their next sign-in.

## Decision

- **The admission hook.** `BuildingBlocks.Application/Actors` declares `IPersonAdmission` (`AdmitAsync(SignedInPerson, CancellationToken)` and `IsAdmittedAsync(Guid objectId, CancellationToken)`, both answering whether the person may hold a session), `SignedInPerson(ObjectId, Name, UserName)` and `AppRoles` (`User` = `Erp.User`, `Administrator` = `Erp.Admin`, the values `app-roles.json` registers, both for people only, checked by `AppRolesTests`). `BuildingBlocks.Authentication` calls the hook; the module `Identity/Users` implements it (`UserAdmission`, registered scoped by `UsersModule.AddServices`).
- **At sign-in.** `SessionCookieEvents.SigningIn` admits the person before it stamps the session or records its Entra session. It first makes the new principal the request's user, so the audit columns of a record the sign-in creates name the person. `AdmitPersonCommand` takes the record linked to the object id, else a record with the same normalised work email (`preferred_username`, trimmed, lower case) and no object id yet, which it links, else creates an active record (`User.FirstSignIn`). An active record takes the name and work email of the ID token and the sign-in time (`LastSignedInAt`); a deactivated one admits nobody, and `SigningIn` throws `SignInRefusedException`. `SignInEvents.TicketReceived` then signs the cookie scheme out again, logs the refusal at `Warning`, records `SecurityEventKind.SignInRefused` with the detail `deactivated` (stored as 7 in the existing `tinyint` column, so no migration) and redirects to `AuthPaths.AccountDeactivatedPage` (`/login?error=account-deactivated`). The sign-in page shows "Account deactivated" with a destructive alert for that state.
- **Concurrent sign-ins.** Two sign-ins of one person can save at once, from two tabs or two browsers. `UserAdmission.AdmitAsync` runs each attempt in a DI scope of its own and tries again after `concurrency.conflict`, which the unit of work answers for a stale row version and for a concurrent first sign-in that created the record first (next bullet). After the fifth attempt it only reads whether the record admits the person, because the sign-ins that kept winning brought it up to date. Any other failure throws, so the sign-in fails like any session that cannot be started (`/login?error=sign-in-failed`) and nobody is admitted on a record that could not be read or written.
- **Duplicates saved at once.** `EfUnitOfWork` answers a save that a unique index or key refuses (SQL Server errors 2601 and 2627) with the same `Error.Conflict("concurrency.conflict", …)` as a stale row version, naming the entity types: the handler looked for the duplicate before a concurrent transaction committed it. Two administrators registering the same work email or employee code at the same moment get one 201 and one 409, instead of a 500, and the unique indexes keep the table right either way.
- **On every request.** `ValidatePrincipal` accepts a cookie only while `IsAdmittedAsync(oid)` holds: an active record, not deleted, linked to the object id (`IsPersonAdmittedQuery`, one indexed read). It runs after the revocation and Entra session checks and before the token cache read, so a refused cookie never slides the token cache entry. A deactivated or deleted person's next request with any of their cookies is refused like any other refused cookie: 401, the session and antiforgery cookies cleared. `BearerTokenEvents.TokenValidated` refuses a person's token (`BearerTokenClaims.HolderOf` is `Person`) whose holder is not admitted, with `invalid_token` and a `BearerTokenRefused` event with the reason `not-admitted`. An application's token is not checked against people. A record that cannot be read fails the request (500) and leaves the cookie as it is, as every other session record does.
- **The module.** `backend/Modules/Identity/Users`, schema `identity_users`, table `Users`: `Id` (`UserId`, version 7 GUID), `EntraObjectId` (null until the first sign-in of a person registered by work email), `DisplayName` (256), `WorkEmail` (254, lower case), `EmployeeCode` (32), `PhoneNumber` (`varchar(16)`, E.164), `Designation` (100), `DateOfJoining` (`date`), `Status` (`tinyint`: 1 active, 2 deactivated), `LastSignedInAt`, the audit and soft-delete columns and `RowVersion`. The unique indexes cover only records not deleted: `EntraObjectId` where it is set, `EmployeeCode` where it is set, and `WorkEmail` only where `EntraObjectId` is null, because a first sign-in matches by email only those records. A linked record is matched by its object id alone, so Entra reassigning an address to a new person neither blocks that person's sign-in nor links them to the earlier holder's record.
- **What a record holds.** Entra owns the name and the work email: every sign-in refreshes them, and the API never edits them. Administrators keep the employee code (letters, digits, `-`, `/` and `_`, starting with a letter or a digit), the phone number (stored as E.164; a number without `+` is read as a ten-digit Indian mobile number starting with 6, 7, 8 or 9, with an optional leading 0, and gets `+91`), the designation and the date of joining.
- **The routes.** `/api/identity/users` (flag `Erp.Modules.Identity.Users`): `GET /` (paged; sort `displayName`, `workEmail`, `employeeCode`, `designation`, `dateOfJoining`, `status`, `lastSignedInAt`, `createdAt`, by `displayName` when none is given; filter `displayName`, `workEmail`, `employeeCode`, `designation`, `dateOfJoining` and `status`), `GET /{id}`, `POST /` (idempotency key, 201 with `Location`), `PUT /{id}` (the four administered details and `version`), `PUT /{id}/status` (`status` `active` or `deactivated`, and `version`), `DELETE /{id}` (soft delete, 204). Every route requires the app role `Erp.Admin`, answering 403 to anyone else, until `user-management-roles-and-permissions` replaces the role with the declared permissions `identity.users.people.{read,register,update,change-status,delete}` (`UsersPermissions`). A change answers the record as saved, read again, with its new `version` (the base64 of its row version); a stale version answers 409 `concurrency.conflict`, one the API never issued 400 `user.version-invalid`. Registration refuses an object id, work email or employee code another record holds (409 `user.entra-object-id-taken`, `user.work-email-taken`, `user.employee-code-taken`, where the work email is compared with every record, linked or not), and an administrator can change neither the status nor the existence of their own record (422 `user.own-status`, `user.own-deletion`).
- **Deleted records.** A deleted record stays in the table with its audit columns and admits nobody. The next sign-in of a person who still holds an app role creates a new record, which an administrator describes again.
- **The flag gates the routes only.** Admission runs whatever `Erp.Modules.Identity.Users` says, so turning off the administration of people never lets a deactivated person back in. The flag is seeded enabled under both labels (`infra/appconfig/feature-flags.json`).
- **Test sign-ins.** `TestSignIn` and the end-to-end host's `PersonaSignIn` start sessions through the same cookie sign-in, so each test person gets a record through the real admission. `TestSignIn` also signs in people admitted to `TestTokenEndpoint` (`TestTokenEndpoint.Find`), which the host tests use for people of their own (`PersonRecords`), so a test that deactivates or deletes a record never touches a persona other tests share.
- **Earlier decisions this changes.**
  - ADR-0016: the unit of work also answers a duplicate saved at once with `concurrency.conflict`.
  - ADR-0031: a session starts only for an admitted person, and the sign-in page has a second error state, `account-deactivated`.
  - ADR-0032 and ADR-0036: the session check also reads the person's record on every request.
  - ADR-0034: a person's bearer token is refused while their record does not admit them.
  - ADR-0035: every persona sign-in creates a record.
  - ADR-0038: the security event kind `SignInRefused`.
  - The roadmap scope: the schema is `identity_users`, and no administrator is bootstrapped from configuration, because the registration's app role assignment makes one. The per-request check of `user-management-user-lifecycle`'s scope exists from this item on; that item keeps the offboarding hook and the directory sync.

## Consequences

- Every signed-in request costs one more indexed read, besides the sign-out time, the Entra session and the token cache, and fails with 500, as they do, while the database cannot answer it.
- Deactivating or deleting a person ends every session they hold, on every device, at their next request; their Entra account, their app role assignment and their sessions in other applications stay as they are.
- Removing a person's app role assignment in Entra stops their next sign-in at Entra, but leaves an open ERP session until it ends (30 minutes idle, 12 hours at most) unless an administrator also deactivates the record here; `user-management-user-lifecycle` flags such accounts through the directory sync.
- Administration is all or nothing through `Erp.Admin` until permissions exist. The API stores no roles, so it cannot tell whether a person it deactivates is the last administrator; an administrator cannot deactivate or delete themselves, but administrators can deactivate each other, and if every administrator's record is deactivated, a record is reactivated in the database.
- A pre-registered record is matched by the `preferred_username` of the person's first sign-in, which for a member of the tenant is their user principal name; an administrator who cannot be sure of it registers the person by object id.
- The designation is free text until `user-management-departments-designations-and-reporting-lines` turns it into a reference.
