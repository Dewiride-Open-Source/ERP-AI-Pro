# ADR-0062: Reference data: seeded lists, every ISO currency, dated exchange rates and financial periods

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-07

## Context

- **The roadmap item** `business-building-blocks-reference-data-module` asks for countries, India's States and Union Territories with their GST State codes, currencies, a dated exchange-rate table with manual entry and file import, April-to-March financial-year and period services, seeding, cached read endpoints and reusable pickers. Its note (from [ADR-0021](0021-migrator-program-and-seeding.md)) seeds reference rows by `ISeeder`, never by migrations.
- **Neighbouring items.** `finance-core-fiscal-calendar-and-year-end-close` owns periods as records (opening balances, role-based locking, the year-end close, tax-year alignment); `finance-core-currency-and-fx` builds INR reporting, gains, losses and revaluation over this item's rate table; `integrations-direct-tax-and-labour-connectors` (Enterprise) owns automated exchange-rate feeds; `business-building-blocks-statutory-parameter-store-and-gst-datasets` lists "State codes" among its datasets.
- **What existed.** `Kernel.Monetary.Currency` was a fixed table of nine currencies (INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD, AUD, CAD, JPY), so `Money` refused every other, and `CurrencyConverter` reads every stored code back through `Currency.FromCode` ([ADR-0015](0015-auditing-soft-delete-and-domain-primitives.md)); `Kernel.Time` had `FinancialYear` and `FinancialQuarter`; `WithReferenceDataCaching(maxAge)` marked answers that are the same for every caller; no product module seeded anything; the web's `Combobox` (ADR-0025) filters the options it is given by their labels; a module's `index.ts` exports components and its navigation alone (I7), so another module cannot call its queries.
- **Research (official sources, read 2026-11-07).**
  - *GST State codes*: GSTN's GSTR-1 template V2.2 (in the GST offline tool) lists 01 to 27, 29 to 38 and 97; no GST list holds 28. Jammu and Kashmir came under GST on 8 July 2017 (Act No. 26 of 2017) and became a Union Territory on 31 October 2019 (Act No. 34 of 2019, S.O. 2889(E)) under the same code; Daman and Diu's 25 served until the transition of Notification 10/2020-Central Tax as amended by 45/2020-Central Tax ended on 31 July 2020 (Circular 168/24/2021-GST); 26 took the merged name on 26 January 2020 (Act No. 44 of 2019, S.O. 4542(E)); Ladakh's 38 applies from 1 January 2020 (Notifications 62/2019 and 3/2020-Central Tax); 97 is the Other Territory (CGST Act s. 2(81), s. 2(114)(f)). 96 is a place of supply outside India (Circular 184/16/2022-GST) and 99 the GSTIN prefix of OIDAR registrations; neither is a State. The Local Government Directory numbers differ (Andhra Pradesh 28, Ladakh 37) and are never GST codes. GSTN's and NIC's site terms forbid redistributing their documents, so the codes are seeded as facts citing CBIC instruments.
  - *Countries*: the GST e-invoice country master (einvoice1.gst.gov.in, Master Codes), which the IRP enforces (error 2202), holds the 249 ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes and XK (Kosovo). ISO press release Ref. 871 allows free use of the ISO 3166 and ISO 4217 codes; no readable official source gives the alpha-3 or numeric codes, and no feature needs them.
  - *Currencies*: ISO 4217 List One as SIX published it on 2026-09-17 holds 178 codes, 155 of them ordinary currencies (10 funds, 4 precious metals, 7 special units, XTS and XXX besides); minor units are 0, 2 or 3. The e-invoice currency master is an older copy: it lacks SLE, VED, XCG and ZWG (IRP error 2203) and still lists six withdrawn codes.
  - *Exchange rates*: CGST rule 34(2) values a supply of services in foreign currency at the rate of generally accepted accounting principles for the date of the time of supply; AS 11 (paras 9 to 11), which a small private company follows, records a transaction at the spot rate of its date, allows an average for a period and restates monetary items at the closing rate; Income-tax Rules, 2026 rule 207 (rule 26 before 1 April 2026) uses the State Bank's telegraphic transfer buying rate on the date tax must be deducted, the last published one when none is that day. FBIL quotes USD, GBP, EUR, AED and RUB per unit and JPY per 100 and IDR per 10,000, to four decimals; CBIC's customs rates now come from ICEGATE; no source offers a stable file to import (FBIL's site renders in a browser and its terms forbid commercial reuse, SBI overwrites one PDF, ICEGATE sits behind a captcha).
- **Owner decisions (2026-11-07).** The Finance role and administrators see, record, import, correct and remove exchange rates, and every employee reads the lists; one rate per currency per day, the books' rate, with the State Bank's buying rate added by the item that deducts tax on payments abroad; the navigation and permission texts approved as written; every ISO currency rather than the nine.

## Decision

- **One Community module `Platform/ReferenceData`**, generated by `node scripts/scaffold/new-module.ts Platform/ReferenceData --entity ExchangeRate --files-only` with the approved texts and reshaped before its first migration: schema `platform_reference_data`, routes under `/api/platform/reference-data`, flag `Erp.Modules.Platform.ReferenceData`, features `Countries`, `IndianStates`, `Currencies`, `FinancialYears` and `ExchangeRates`, change keys `platform.reference-data.countries`, `.indian-states` and `.exchange-rates`.
- **Seeded lists that follow their files.** `Countries` (code, name) and `IndianStates` (a GST State code from a date: name, `TerritoryKind` State, Union Territory or Other Territory, last day, and the instrument that dates it; unique by code and start) are recorded tables filled by `CountrySeeder` and `IndianStateSeeder` from `countries.json` and `indian-states.json`, embedded in the module and read strictly. A seeder makes its table equal to its file: a missing row is added, a changed one follows the file, and one the file does not list is deleted, each change recorded by the system. No person changes these rows, so following the file overwrites nobody's work, and other modules keep a code, never a row, so a deleted code leaves their records whole. Because a code's rows never overlap in the file, the table never holds two rows of one code on one day. This amends ADR-0021, whose seeders only insert what is missing: that rule stays for rows people change. The State codes live here alone; the parameter-store item takes them from this module.
- **Currencies are the kernel's.** `Currency` holds the 155 ordinary currencies of List One with their minor units and English names (`Iso4217Currencies`), so `Money` takes any currency a client pays in; the module serves that list without a table. A code is only ever added, never removed, because every stored amount is read back through `Currency.FromCode`; a withdrawn currency stays for the records that hold it (this amends ADR-0015's fixed table of nine). `CurrencyTests` pins the exact code set. The four ISO currencies the e-invoice master lacks are the e-invoice item's to handle (it leaves the optional foreign-currency fields out for them), with no flag kept here before then.
- **Financial periods are the kernel's.** `FinancialPeriod` is a month of a financial year (number 1 to 12 from April, its quarter, first and last day, label "April 2026"), with `FinancialYear.Periods` and `PeriodOf`. It is the calendar month and nothing more: the fiscal-calendar item's period records, with their locks and opening balances, take another name, because a type of that name in a namespace importing `Kernel.Time` would be ambiguous.
- **Cached reads of whole lists.** `GET /countries`, `/indian-states` (every row, or with `?on=` those in force that day), `/currencies` and `/financial-years?from=&to=` (at most 50 years, each with its quarters and periods) answer whole lists wrapped in an object (the 250 countries exceed the page cap of 200), under `platform.reference-data.lists.read`, held by Employee, kept an hour by the browser (`WithReferenceDataCaching`; the State codes in force change at midnight in India). The server keeps no cache of its own: a web request reads each list once, and a server cache would hide a reseeded list.
- **Exchange rates.** A rate is the rupees for 1, 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000 or 100,000 units of a currency other than INR, kept exactly as published (decimal(19,4), at most four decimals, more than 0 and at most 1,000,000), for today or an earlier day by India's time, with an optional source. It is `IAuditable`, `ISoftDeletable` and `IVersioned`, one undeleted rate per currency and day (a unique index filtered on `[IsDeleted] = 0`, so a removed day can be recorded again). Recording takes an idempotency key and answers 409 for a second rate of a day; an import of 1 to 1,000 rows, which the web parses from the ERP's own CSV template, records all of them or none, leaves a row equal in rate and unit to the recorded one as it is, and refuses every row that breaks a rule, repeats a currency and day of the file or differs from a recorded rate, keyed `rates[i].<field>`, because a correction is made on the rate itself; correcting sends the version; removing soft-deletes. `IExchangeRates.RateOnAsync` answers the latest rate on or before a date with its own date, as business-day sources and rule 207 need. The permissions are `platform.reference-data.exchange-rates.read`, `.record` and `.correct`, held by Finance.
- **Pickers fed by one provider.** The module exports `ReferenceListsProvider`, a server component that reads only the lists a page names (each list on its own, answered `read`, `failed` or `disabled`, the module flag checked first) and hands them to a client context, and the client pickers `CountryPicker`, `IndianStatePicker` (offering the codes in force on its `on` date, without one on today's date in India as the server rendered the page, which the provider passes beside the lists), `CurrencyPicker` (with `excluded` codes), `FinancialYearPicker` and `FinancialPeriodPicker` (whose value is the period's first day) over the `Combobox`. Every label carries its code, and the country, State and currency pickers search code and name through the combobox's `filter`, an item whose code is typed in full first; a chosen value the offered options lack stays chosen (a State code out of force under its latest name and the date, a period under its month, otherwise under its code), so a record keeps what it holds when a list cannot be read or has dropped a code. Another module wraps its form in the provider once and imports only the module's public surface; the harness page `/design/reference-pickers` holds the pickers' component tests. While the module flag is off, every other module's pickers offer nothing and say why.
- **Web pages.** "Reference lists" shows the countries, the GST State codes with their dates and the currencies, each searched and paged on the web server so no page outgrows a full-page capture, and the financial years one at a time through a year chooser, with their quarters and periods; "Exchange rates" lists the rates through the list kit, ten a page, records one, imports a file, and shows a rate with its change history, corrected and removed by the Finance role and administrators.

## Options rejected

- **Seed migrations**: the migrator runs seeders after every migration, and ADR-0021 keeps data out of migrations.
- **Countries and State codes in code alone**: the State codes are statutory facts with dates and instruments, which the project keeps as dated records citing their source, never literals; recorded rows also put every change of a list into the audit trail.
- **A currency table in the module**: two lists of the currencies `Money` accepts would drift apart.
- **A rate kind beside the rate**: the owner keeps one rate a day; the column comes with the item that needs the State Bank's buying rate.
- **Reading the published rate files**: none is stable or free to reuse; the ERP's own template is.
- **Pickers that load their own options through a Server Function**: a read through a Server Function runs after hydration and one at a time; the provider reads on the server with the page.
- **Exchange rates fetched from a feed**: an Enterprise connector by the roadmap.

## Consequences

- A client, vendor or invoice form picks a country, a State code of its document's date, a currency, a financial year and a period from the same lists, and `IIndianStates` lets the clients item check a GSTIN's first two digits against the codes of a date.
- A release that changes a seed file changes every database at its next migrator run, the change visible in the audit trail; `node scripts/ef/ef.ts update --all` applies migrations alone, so a developer runs the migrator to seed.
- Money now holds any of 155 currencies, so code that rounds or formats an amount reads the currency's own minor units, never two.
- `RecordVersion` moved from `Identity/Users` to `BuildingBlocks.Persistence` (`Versioning`), because a second module corrects records by version, and list filters parse a `Currency` field with equality operators.
