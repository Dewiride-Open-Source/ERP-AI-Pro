# ADR-0065: Statutory datasets: provident fund, ESI, gratuity, bonus, professional tax and labour welfare funds

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-08

## Context

- **The roadmap item** `business-building-blocks-statutory-datasets-labour` asks for the labour datasets of the statutory parameter store ([ADR-0063](0063-statutory-parameters-dated-rows-in-versioned-sets-typed-reads-by-date-and-the-first-gst-datasets.md), [ADR-0064](0064-statutory-datasets-tds-under-the-income-tax-act-2025-company-tax-and-depreciation.md)): the provident fund (EPF, EPS and EDLI) and ESI contributions, wage ceilings and rates under the Code on Social Security, 2020 with its schemes and rules, the Codes' wage definition, the States' professional-tax slabs and exemptions, the labour welfare fund contributions by State, and the gratuity and bonus parameters of the Code on Social Security, 2020 and the Code on Wages, 2019.
- **Consumers.** The Enterprise `finance-payroll-pf-esi-pt-and-lwf-engines` and `finance-payroll-full-and-final-and-benefits` compute contributions, professional tax, gratuity and bonus from these sets; `compliance-labour-law-and-posh-registers` and the compliance calendar read their periods and due days.
- **Owner decisions (2026-11-08).** Every dataset starts with the law in force on 1 April 2026 and holds every change to 8 November 2026, changes already notified for a later day included. Professional tax, the labour welfare fund and the States' bonus limits cover every State: a State whose instrument could not be read gets no rows and is named in the item's note, and no set records that a State levies no tax or keeps no fund unless an official text says so. Minimum wages are their own Community item, `business-building-blocks-statutory-datasets-minimum-wages`, before the payroll work. The States that have notified no bonus limits of their own take the repealed Payment of Bonus Act's ₹21,000 and ₹7,000 as the ERP's reading of the Code on Wages, s. 69(2); ESI's coverage limits and its ₹176 exemption, which no instrument has held since 8 May 2026, are not filled; the bonus sets answer from the accounting year 2026-27; the owner downloads in a browser the official documents the research tools could not open, for a later reading.
- **Research (official sources, read 2026-11-08).** The Gazette of India (egazette.gov.in), EPFO's and ESIC's consolidated schemes, rules and regulations, and the States' Gazettes, Acts and department pages; India Code, the Ministry of Labour and Employment, PIB and several State sites refuse automated fetching or fail their certificates.
  - *The Codes.* The Code on Social Security, 2020 and the Code on Wages, 2019 came into force on 21 November 2025 (S.O. 5319(E) as corrected by S.O. 5936(E) of 19 December 2025; S.O. 5322(E)); s. 164 of the former and s. 69 of the latter repeal the earlier Acts and keep their rules, schemes, regulations and notifications for a time. Both define wages alike: the excluded items above one-half of all remuneration are added back, and remuneration in kind counts up to 15 % (s. 2(88); s. 2(y)).
  - *Provident fund.* The 1952, 1995 and 1976 schemes ran on under s. 164 until the Employees' Provident Funds, Pension and Deposit Linked Insurance Schemes, 2026 (G.S.R. 525(E), 527(E) and 526(E)) from 29 June 2026; the Chapter III wage ceiling is ₹15,000 (S.O. 2702(E), 29 May 2026) and ₹25,000 from 17 September 2026 (S.O. 5109(E)), the Central Government's 1.16 % staying on wages up to ₹15,000 (G.S.R. 890(E)); 12 % or, for the classes of S.O. 3582(E), 10 %; EPS 8.33 %, EDLI 0.5 %; administrative charges 0.5 % (S.O. 2011(E)) and inspection charges of exempted funds (S.O. 2701(E), corrected by S.O. 4602(E)).
  - *ESI.* The Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026 (G.S.R. 344(E), 8 May 2026) superseded the ESI (Central) Rules, 1950: rule 19 keeps 3¼ and ¾ per cent, rounded to the next higher rupee, and holds neither the ₹21,000 and ₹25,000 coverage limits nor the ₹176 exemption, which no later instrument holds; the ESI (General) Regulations, 1950 that fix the contribution periods and the payment day last a year from 21 November 2025 (s. 164(2)(b)). ESIC's consolidated rule 51 still prints the replaced rates, so the rates of 1 April to 7 May 2026 are unread.
  - *Gratuity and bonus.* Chapter V of the Code on Social Security (the ₹20 lakh ceiling of S.O. 1420(E) kept by s. 164(2)(a); a fixed-term worker, as s. 2(zr) of the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 defines one, after one year (s. 2(o)(c)), any other fixed-term employee under the Code on Social Security's own rules) and Chapter IV of the Code on Wages with the Wages (Central) Rules, 2026 (G.S.R. 343(E)); the Central Government's limits are ₹21,000 and ₹7,000 or the minimum wage (S.O. 4711(E), S.O. 4710(E) of 25 August 2026, deemed from 21 November 2025). No State's own notification under s. 26 was read; Bihar's (S.O. 241 and 239 of 30 October 2026) is known only from a pointer.
  - *Professional tax and the labour welfare funds.* Read in their instruments: the slabs of Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka, Telangana, West Bengal (to 30 September 2026; the final Schedule 1607-F.T. that applies from 1 October 2026 is on no official host), Assam, Tripura, Bihar, Mizoram, Nagaland and Punjab; Odisha's repeal from 1 April 2026 (Ordinance 02 of 2026) and Jammu and Kashmir's certificate that no such tax is charged; the welfare funds of Delhi, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka, Kerala, Goa, Odisha, Haryana and Telangana.

## Decision

- **No composite keys.** A labour set keeps one key, in the instrument's own notation unless it is a provident fund or ESI set (below): a `code` key, a State's entry written `<GST State code>-<entry>` (`27-1(i)(b)` for Maharashtra's Schedule I entry 1(i)(b)) beside a `stateCode` column that the reference data checks, or the `stateCode` column itself where a set holds one entry per State (`professional-tax.levies`, `bonus.state-wage-limit-readings`): a slab whose bound changes stays the same entry and shows as changed, where a key holding the bound would show it removed and added.
- **Amounts in paise.** An `amount` holds at most two decimals (`ParameterColumn.AmountMaxDecimals`), never negative: Delhi's labour welfare fund charges an employee 75 paise and an employer ₹2.25 a half-year.
- **A `dayMonth` cell kind**: canonical `MM-dd`, a JSON string in a seed file, read as the Contracts type `DayAndMonth`, never a key and without bounds; 29 February is accepted, a day of leap years, so a consumer places a day in a year before it counts with it.
- **Five as-of meanings**: `wageDate` (a day for which wages are earned: the provident fund and the wage definition; a month a version splits is split by the payroll engine as EPFO's instructions say), `wagePeriodEnd` (the last day of a wage period: ESI, whose contribution for a wage period falls due on its last day, s. 29(4)), `employmentEndDate` (the day employment ends: gratuity), `accountingYearEnd` (the last day of the accounting year a bonus is for) and `contributionPeriodEnd` (the last day of a contribution period, the register day: the labour welfare fund). Professional tax keeps `taxPeriodStart`, the salary month or year.
- **State names on the pages.** `ParameterCellResponse.stateName` names the State or Union territory that a GST State code cell names on the entry's first day (`IIndianStates`, read only for a set with a `stateCode` column); the pages show `Maharashtra (27)` and find an entry by its State's name.
- **Twenty-four sets**, each a seed file under `Statutory/Labour/<Area>/` and a typed record under `Contracts/Labour/<Area>/`, from 1 April 2026 unless a set's first instrument came later:
  - provident fund, as of `wageDate`: `provident-fund.contribution-rates`, `-ten-per-cent-establishments`, `-membership-limits` (the wage ceiling and the pay limits of membership), `-charges`, `-due-dates`, `-rounding`;
  - ESI, as of `wagePeriodEnd`: `esi.contribution-rates` (from 8 May 2026), `-contribution-exemptions`, `-average-daily-wage-divisors`, `-coverage-wage-limits`, `-contribution-periods`, `-contribution-due-dates`;
  - `gratuity.rules`, as of `employmentEndDate`; `bonus.rules`, `bonus.wage-limits` (the Central Government's), `bonus.state-wage-limit-readings` (the ERP's reading for every State and Union territory but Bihar) and `bonus.prior-charges` (from 8 May 2026), as of `accountingYearEnd`; `labour-codes.wage-definition`, as of `wageDate`;
  - professional tax, as of `taxPeriodStart`: `professional-tax.levies` (who levies it, or that a text says no State tax is levied), `-salary-slabs` and `-exemptions`;
  - the labour welfare fund, as of `contributionPeriodEnd`: `labour-welfare-fund.contributions`, `-due-days` and `-coverage`.
- **Descriptive keys for the provident fund and ESI.** Their entries are keyed by what they are (`provident-fund-employer`, `pension-fund-employer`, `monthly-rated`, `employer-share-disability`), not by clause, because many pass from the 1952, 1995 and 1976 schemes or the Employees' State Insurance (Central) Rules, 1950 to the 2026 ones under new clauses: the source names the clause of each version, so the change of 29 June or 8 May 2026 shows such an entry changed rather than removed and added. `provident-fund.ten-per-cent-establishments`, one notification's list, keeps its paragraph numbers.
- **Exact fractions.** The bonus's "eight and one-third per cent." is held as 25/3 in two integer columns, because a percent holds four decimals; the pension share stays 8.33, the figure the paragraph that directs the remittance writes.
- **Rows that end with what holds them.** ESI's contribution periods and payment day end on 20 November 2026 by a version from 21 November 2026, the earlier reading of a year from 21 November 2025, so the store never claims a regulation on a day it may have lapsed; Haryana's welfare fund limit ends on 31 December 2026, because its Act re-indexes it every 1 January; West Bengal's slabs end on 30 September 2026. A version without them means that the instrument for the days after is not yet seeded, never that nothing is due.

## Options rejected

- **Composite keys**: the instruments number their entries, so one key in their notation is the entry's identity across versions.
- **One as-of meaning for every labour set**: the Codes tie each figure to a different day (a day of work, the end of a wage period, the end of employment, of an accounting year or of a contribution period), and one meaning would answer some of them on the wrong day.
- **Seeding ESI's ₹21,000 and ₹176 after 7 May 2026 from ESIC's practice**: no instrument holds them, and the store holds only what an instrument says (owner decision).
- **Seeding from search snippets, payroll sites and department summaries**: they are not the instruments, and they contradict them in places.

## Consequences

- The payroll engine splits a wage month that a version divides, asks ESIC's instruction for coverage while no Chapter IV ceiling is notified, and reads a State's bonus limits from the ERP's reading until the State's own notification is seeded.
- The employer's limits of rule 33(2) of the Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026 for a gratuity application run from the day it is received, which no set of employment-end days answers, so they wait for the compliance calendar.
- A release follows each change these sets end on: the final ESI (General) Regulations, 2026 (to check before 20 November 2026), West Bengal's 1607-F.T., Haryana's limit for 2027, and each State's own bonus notification.
- The documents the research tools could not open (refused or failed certificates, India Code, the Kolkata and Bihar Gazettes) are read from the owner's downloads in a later round; until then their States have no rows and are named in the item's note.
- **Earlier decisions this changes.** ADR-0063: an amount holds paise, the cell kinds gain `dayMonth` and the as-of dates five labour meanings, and a cell of a GST State code carries its State's name. ADR-0064: its rejected composite keys stay rejected for the labour sets.
