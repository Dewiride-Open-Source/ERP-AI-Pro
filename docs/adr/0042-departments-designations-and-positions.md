# ADR-0042: Departments, designations and positions

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-11

## Context

- **What existed.** ADR-0039 gave each person's record a free-text designation (`identity_users.Users.Designation`, at most 100 characters), to be turned into a reference by `user-management-departments-designations-and-reporting-lines`. Nothing recorded departments or who reports to whom.
- **The roadmap scope** asked for a department tree, designations, manager assignment with effective dates, position history and an org-chart query, exposed through admin endpoints under `/api/identity/organisation`, consumed by approvals and timesheets later and extended, not replaced, by `hr-core`.
- **Owner decisions of 2026-10-11:**
  - only administrators change departments, designations and reporting lines; HR can be given this later by a change in code;
  - everyone signed in reads the departments, the designations and today's organisation chart (names, designations, departments and managers); a person's history of moves, and so the chart of any other date, stays with those who can change it;
  - at any date a person is in at most one department and reports to at most one manager;
  - a change may start on any date, past or future: a past date corrects a change entered late, a future one plans a move.
- **Earlier owner decision (2026-10-10):** the free-text designations become entries of the designation list.

## Decision

- **Placement.** Departments, designations and positions are feature folders of `Identity/Users` (`Departments/`, `Designations/`, `Positions/`), in the schema `identity_users`, under `/api/identity/users`. A separate module `Identity/Organisation` would need one module's migration to read another module's table, or a two-step migration, to turn `Users.Designation` into designations; the first breaks the module boundary (root CLAUDE.md §5) and the second the no-compatibility rule (§8). In one context the rules that span people and their positions also stay in one transaction: a manager is a person whose record is not deleted, a person someone reports to is not deleted, and the people list sorts and filters by the current position in SQL. The phase goal already places departments and designations under user management. This replaces the route wording of the roadmap scope. What two features share lives in the module's `Shared/`, because a feature never reaches into another: `Shared/Positions` (`CountingPositions`, `PeopleWithPositions`, `PersonRow`), which the people, departments, designations and positions all read, and `Shared/Versioning` (`RecordVersion`, the `version` of a record on the wire).
- **Departments.** `Department` (`DepartmentId`, `IAuditable`, `ISoftDeletable`, `IVersioned`): `Name` (trimmed, 1 to 100 characters) and `ParentId` (none at the top). The table `Departments` keeps a unique index on (`ParentId`, `Name`) filtered to rows not deleted; SQL Server treats NULLs as equal in it, so two top-level departments cannot share a name either, and the filter is spelled out because EF Core would otherwise leave rows without a parent out. The tree is not dated: a rename or a move holds for every date. A move under the department itself or one within it is refused (422 `department.would-contain-itself`, `DepartmentTree`).
- **Designations.** `Designation` (`DesignationId`, the same interfaces): `Name` (1 to 100 characters), unique among those not deleted. A rename holds for every position that names it.
- **Positions.** `Position` (`PositionId`, `IAuditable`; history, so neither soft-deletable nor ever edited): `UserId`, `EffectiveFrom` (`date`), `DepartmentId?`, `DesignationId?`, `ManagerId?`. A row is the person's whole position from its date until the date their next row starts; an empty one ends what the previous row named. The position a person holds on a date is their latest row starting on or before it, and today is `IndianStandardTime.Today`, so a future row becomes current at midnight without a write and no column stores the current position. A unique index on (`UserId`, `EffectiveFrom`) keeps one row per person and date (409 `position.date-taken`). A correction withdraws a row and records another. A row keeps the names of a department, designation or manager deleted since: the queries that name them (`PeopleWithPositions`, `PositionsWithNames`) read through `IncludeDeleted()`, which takes any entity type (`SoftDeleteFilter` in `BuildingBlocks.Persistence`), because ignoring the named filter covers every entity a query reads, whatever its root; the people themselves are then filtered by `IsDeleted` by hand.
- **Rules of a position.** A person never manages themself (422 `position.self-managed`). The department, designation and manager exist and are not deleted (404 `position.department-not-found`, `position.designation-not-found`, `position.manager-not-found`: an id that names nothing answers 404 with its own code, as `directory.person-not-found` does). A manager whose record is deactivated is refused only for a row that holds today or later (422 `position.manager-inactive`), because someone who left was active then. No reporting cycle closes on any date a row holds (422 `position.reporting-cycle`): the chain of managers changes only on a date some row starts, and a change to one person's rows can close a cycle only through that person, so `ReportingLines.ClosesCycle` walks their chain on the first date the change holds and on every later row date until their next row. Withdrawing a row hands its dates to the row before it, so that row is checked again when it then holds today or later (422 `position.previous-no-longer-valid`) and the cycle check runs for the dates it takes over.
- **What still counts.** A department, designation or manager is in use while a row that counts names it (`CountingPositions`): a row of a person whose record is not deleted that holds today or starts later. A row a later one replaced on or before today is history, and the last row of a deleted person, which never ends, holds for nobody. A department in use or with departments within it is not deleted (409 `department.in-use`), nor a designation in use (409 `designation.in-use`), nor a person someone reports to (409 `user.manages-people`).
- **Serialised writes.** These rules read what other writes change, so every write to the tree or the positions, and every delete of something a row names (a department, a designation, a person), takes the exclusive application lock `identity_users.organisation` of its transaction first (`OrganisationLock`, over `TransactionLocks.TryAcquireExclusiveAsync` in `BuildingBlocks.Persistence/UnitOfWork`, `sp_getapplock` with `@LockOwner = 'Transaction'`). The unit of work opens the transaction before the handler runs, the lock ends with it, and a retry of the execution strategy takes it again; a lock not granted within 10 seconds answers 409 `concurrency.conflict`. Creating and renaming a designation take none: the unique index answers a duplicate saved at the same moment.
- **Routes and permissions.** Under `/api/identity/users` (`docs/routing.md`): departments and designations (list, get, create with an idempotency key, update with the record's version, delete), a person's positions (`GET /{id}/positions`, `GET /{id}/positions/{positionId}`, `POST /{id}/positions` with an idempotency key, `DELETE /{id}/positions/{positionId}`) and `GET /org-chart?on=`. Their route names take the form of the module's other routes, `Identity.Users.<Action>` (`Identity.Users.ListDepartments`, `Identity.Users.RecordPosition`, `Identity.Users.GetOrgChart`). Every list is paged, the chart included, because the convention caps every list. Employee holds `identity.users.departments.read`, `identity.users.designations.read` and `identity.users.org-chart.read`; administrators alone hold the rest (`create`, `update` and `delete` of departments and designations, `identity.users.positions.read`, `.record` and `.withdraw`). The chart of a date other than today needs `identity.users.positions.read` as well, checked through `ICallerPermissions` (403 `request.forbidden`), because it shows where people were or will be. The chart holds the people whose record is active now and who have joined by the date, that is with no date of joining or one on or before it, so a planned hire appears from their joining date; a person's status is not dated, so a past chart leaves out someone deactivated since, and a person whose manager is deactivated names a manager outside the chart.
- **People.** `User.Designation` is gone: registration, invitation and `PUT /{id}` no longer take a designation, and `PersonResponse` carries `departmentId`, `departmentName`, `designationId`, `designationName`, `managerId` and `managerDisplayName` of the position held today, as plain fields, because a nullable object would need `oneOf`, which `OpenApiSchemaShapeTests` refuses. The people list sorts by `department`, `designation` and `manager` and filters by `departmentId`, `designationId` and `managerId`, through `PeopleWithPositions`: one query with an `OUTER APPLY` of the current row, projected with an object initializer because EF Core composes a filter or a sort over a projection only through members it assigned.
- **The conversion.** The migration `AddDepartmentsDesignationsAndPositions` creates the three tables, then inserts one designation for each distinct trimmed, non-empty `Users.Designation`, deleted records included so no text is lost, and a text only deleted records held comes in as a deleted designation, kept for their history and named by no new position (the database's case-insensitive collation groups case variants; `MIN(... COLLATE Latin1_General_BIN2)` picks one spelling deterministically; `NEWID()` ids, which nothing requires to be version 7; created by `ActorIds.System`), and one position per such record with that designation from its date of joining, or else the date in India it was created, and never later than the day the migration runs, so a person who had a designation still shows it today. Then it drops the column. Its data steps run through `EXEC(N'...')`, because an idempotent script compiles the whole batch, and SQL Server defers name resolution for missing tables only, not for a column dropped already. The Down migration restores as text the designation of the position each person holds on the day it runs, and none when that position names no designation or the person holds none yet. `DesignationConversionTests` migrate an empty database to the migration before, write such records, and run the conversion and its Down.

## Consequences

- Approvals and timesheets resolve a person's manager on a date from `Positions`; the first of them adds the contract it needs to `Identity.Users.Contracts`.
- `hr-core-employee-master-extension` keeps HR's own dated employment details in its module and reads departments, designations and managers from here.
- The organisation is one lock wide: two administrators' changes to it are saved one after the other. That is negligible for the company's size and keeps the cycle and in-use rules exact.
- A department tree that changes over time, department heads, designation grades and matrix reporting are not modelled.
- The web pages for departments, designations and the chart arrive with `user-management-web-invitations-organisation-and-my-profile`, and the people pages with `user-management-web-users-roles-and-permissions`.
