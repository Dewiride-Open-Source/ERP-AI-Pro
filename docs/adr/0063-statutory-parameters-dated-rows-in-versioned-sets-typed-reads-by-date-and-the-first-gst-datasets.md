# ADR-0063: Statutory parameters: dated rows in versioned sets, typed reads by date and the first GST datasets

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-07

## Context

- **The roadmap item** `business-building-blocks-statutory-parameter-store-and-gst-datasets` asks for effective-dated, versioned parameter sets seeded from `statutory/*.json`, an `IStatutoryParameters.GetAsOf(date)` service, list and read endpoints, an administrator's viewer with a version diff, and the first GST datasets pinned by golden-value tests. Root `CLAUDE.md` §5 already requires every statutory rate, threshold, slab, section number and due date to be an effective-dated parameter record with its government source, never a literal in code. The GST State codes are `Platform/ReferenceData`'s ([ADR-0062](0062-reference-data-seeded-lists-every-iso-currency-dated-exchange-rates-and-financial-periods.md)), read through `IIndianStates`; this item never copies them.
- **Consumers.** `finance-core-gst-tax-engine` resolves rates by date; `compliance-compliance-calendar-engine` derives obligations per tax period and State; `business-building-blocks-statutory-datasets-direct-tax-and-labour` adds TDS sections with the ITA-1961 to ITA-2025 crosswalk, per-year thresholds, company tax rates and surcharge, the advance-tax schedule, professional-tax State slabs, PF and ESI ceilings and rates, LWF, gratuity, bonus and Schedule II lives, all tables of keyed rows whose values change from dated instruments; the Enterprise `compliance-ai-compliance-assistant` writes a "what changed" digest from parameter-set diffs; `finance-core-web-finance-admin` lists a "statutory parameter sets" screen.
- **Owner decisions (2026-11-07).** The page and its one permission are the Finance role's and administrators'. Statutory changes reach the ERP only with a release: the seed files change with each notification, and no person edits a rate, code or due date. This item's datasets: the SAC codes of heading 9983 (its 9 groups and 59 six-digit codes) and 997331 with heading 9973 and group 99733, including heading 9973's rewording from 1 October 2019; the CGST, IGST and UTGST rate entries that cover those codes, with their full history; the standing GST return due-date rules for tax periods from January 2021 (GSTR-1 monthly and quarterly with IFF, GSTR-3B monthly and quarterly by State list, PMT-06, GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C) and the turnover limits that decide QRMP eligibility, the IFF and GSTR-9C. One-off due-date extensions belong to the calendar engine and each State's own SGST notification to `finance-core-company-profile`. The navigation area "Compliance", the entry "Statutory parameters" with its description and the permission sentence were approved as written. After the design was reviewed the owner decided, the same day, that golden-value tests of a statutory dataset may write each pinned value beside its instrument (the one exception to root `CLAUDE.md` §5's "never a literal in code or tests"), that the ERP's reading of each SAC code's rate entry ships now, badged as such, and that the GSTR-9 exemption is not seeded at all while the notification that carries it from 2024-25 is unread.
- **Research (official sources, read 2026-11-07).**
  - *Rates.* Each tax is levied by its own notification stating only its own rate: 11/2017-Central Tax (Rate) (G.S.R. 690(E)), 8/2017-Integrated Tax (Rate) (683(E)) and 11/2017-Union Territory Tax (Rate) (702(E)), all in force 1 July 2017, with one serial numbering; no statute derives one rate from another. Their amendment chains are complete to 06/2026-CTR (27 October 2026, in force 1 November 2026, touching none of these entries). Heading 9983's residual item 21(ii) has been 9 % CGST, 18 % IGST, 9 % UTGST throughout, its wording changed on 1 January 2019, 1 October 2019 and 22 September 2025; item 21(i) (print-media advertising space) is 2.5/5/2.5; item 21(ia) (services relating to petroleum or natural-gas exploration) was 6/12/6 from 1 October 2019 until its omission from 22 September 2025 (15/2025-CTR, G.S.R. 663(E)); entry 38 (renewable-energy plant set-up, headings 9954, 9983 or 9987) is 9/18/9 from 1 January 2019; item 17(ii), which carries SAC 997331, is 9/18/9 throughout, reworded on 1 October 2021 (6/2021-CTR). The notifications work by heading and description, never by six-digit code, so linking a code to an entry is interpretation. The Gazette numbers of 27/2018-CTR, 28/2018-ITR and 27/2018-UTTR were not located; the UTGST copy of 3/2022 (the "print media" definition) was not opened; entry 38's Explanation after 2021 was not quoted for IGST and UTGST; entry 39's UTGST counterpart was not read. Compensation cess on these services is Nil (2/2017-Compensation Cess (Rate) S.No. 3).
  - *SAC codes.* The Annexure (Scheme of Classification of Services) to 11/2017-CTR is the legal source; of its three amendments only 20/2019-CTR touches the codes in scope (heading 9973 from "with or without operator" to "without operator" from 1 October 2019). GSTN's `HSN_SAC.xlsx` lacks the five-digit groups, adds 998329, 998389 and 998398 and keeps omitted codes, so it is not a faithful copy of the law; the Explanatory Notes are classification guidance with titles that differ from the Annexure.
  - *Due dates.* GSTR-1 11th (83/2020-Central Tax, paragraph 1) and 13th for a quarter (its first proviso); IFF from the 1st to the 13th for the first two months of a quarter (rule 59(2) as substituted by 82/2020-CT); GSTR-3B 20th (rule 61(1)(i)) and, for a quarter, 22nd or 24th by the two State lists of rule 61(1)(ii); PMT-06 25th (rule 61(3)); GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C 31 December after the financial year (rule 80(1) and (3) as substituted by 30/2021-CT from 1 August 2021, governing years from 2020-21); QRMP up to ₹5 crore in the preceding year (84/2020-CT); IFF up to ₹50 lakh a month; GSTR-9C above ₹5 crore; the GSTR-9 exemption up to ₹2 crore by a notification per year (10/2022, 32/2023 and 14/2024-CT for 2021-22 to 2023-24). Unconfirmed: the merged Union Territory (code 26), which the rule never names, and the Other Territory (97), which neither list holds; the exemption from 2024-25 (15/2025-CT, known only from a GST Council newsletter); every Central Tax notification after 22 April 2026, which no index the research could read covered.
- **Constraints.** The OpenAPI document may use no `oneOf`, `anyOf`, `allOf`, `not` or discriminator; a folder holds at most 12 source files; every mapped entity is `IAuditable` or `IAppendOnly` with a change key; SQL Server's `json` type may round a JSON number converted from text (learn.microsoft.com, "JSON data type", "Numeric precision").

## Decision

- **One Community module `Compliance/StatutoryParameters`**, generated by `node scripts/scaffold/new-module.ts Compliance/StatutoryParameters --entity ParameterSet --files-only` with the approved texts and reshaped to read only before its first migration: schema `compliance_statutory_parameters`, routes under `/api/compliance/statutory-parameters`, flag `Erp.Modules.Compliance.StatutoryParameters`, one permission `compliance.statutory-parameters.parameter-sets.read` held by Finance, change keys `compliance.statutory-parameters.parameter-sets`, `.parameter-versions` and `.parameter-rows`. The generated create permission, command and form never ship.
- **A parameter set is data, not a type.** Three tables serve every dataset: `ParameterSets` (key, title, description, basis `law` or `interpretation`, the meaning of its as-of date, and its declared columns), `ParameterVersions` (the date a version applies from and the instruments that brought it, unique per set) and `ParameterRows` (a row key, the dates it is in force, its cells and the source that gave it its current text and value, unique by set, key and start). Each is an `IAuditable` record no person changes, deleted by the seeder when its file no longer holds it, its versions and rows filed under their set.
- **A version is a complete snapshot by meaning and a delta by storage.** A seed file declares its versions and dates each row from a version's date until the day before a later one's. The version in force on a date is the latest one from on or before it; its content is every row in force that day; its changes are the rows that start on its date and those that end the day before, matched by key (added, changed, removed). Versions are strictly increasing and run until the next one, so after the first there is no gap and no overlap; rows of one key never overlap and may stop and return; every version cites at least one instrument and changes at least one row; a version may lie in the future. A correction of the ERP's copy edits a row in place and shows only in the audit trail; a legal change adds a version.
- **The as-of date is declared per set.** `supplyDate` for the rates, the SAC codes and the ERP's reading of their entries: a version's date is the day its instrument came into force, and the store answers for the date of the supply (which date that is, and section 14's change of rate, are the tax engine's). `taxPeriodStart` for the due dates, the State lists and the limits: a version's date is the first day of the first tax period it governs, which may precede its instrument (an exemption notified after the year it covers), and the store answers for the first day of a tax period. Before a set's first version the store answers nothing; on a covered date it answers the rows in force, which may be none.
- **Columns, keys and kinds are declared in the seed file**, stored on the set and drawn by one viewer: camelCase names with titles; exactly one key column (`code` or `stateCode`), whose value is the row key; an optional row-title column; kinds `text`, `code`, `integer`, `percent`, `amount` (rupees), `choice` (declared values with titles) and `stateCode`; `optional`, `minimum` and `maximum`; and, in the seed file only, `references`, the sets whose row key a code must be on every day of the row, which the reader checks and nothing stores or serves. Cells are stored as a JSON object of canonical text per column (never a JSON number), through value objects compared by value, so a reseed of equal content writes nothing. New kinds and composite keys come with the sets that need them.
- **Seed files are read strictly and checked whole before anything is written.** One file per set, `ParameterSets/Persistence/Statutory/<area>/<key>.json`, embedded as `statutory/<key>.json`, its name equal to its key; read with `RespectNullableAnnotations`, `RespectRequiredConstructorParameters`, `UnmappedMemberHandling.Disallow`, `AllowDuplicateProperties = false`, `NumberHandling = JsonNumberHandling.Strict` and camelCase enums only; every cell checked by its kind, every date rule above, every reference covered by the referenced rows, and every `stateCode` covered by that code's rows of `IIndianStates`. `StatutoryParameterSeeder` (order 200, after the reference data's seeders 100 and 110) makes the tables equal to the files, matching sets by key, versions by set and date and rows by set, key and start, each change recorded by the system (ADR-0062's rule for rows no person changes). A defective file stops the migrator, and with it the deployment, before any write.
- **Typed reads in Contracts.** A set record names its key as a static abstract member, as an integration event names itself, and builds itself from its version and rows: `IStatutoryParameterSet<TSelf>` (`static abstract string Key`, `static abstract TSelf Create(StatutoryParameterVersion, IStatutoryParameterRows)`), `IStatutoryParameterRows.Read<TRow>()` (implemented in the module: canonical cells rendered by kind, `percent` as `Percentage`, `amount` as `Money` in INR, read strictly) and `IStatutoryParameters.GetAsOfAsync<TSet>(DateOnly, CancellationToken)`. This item's records: `SacCodes`, `SacRateEntries`, `CentralTaxRates`, `IntegratedTaxRates` and `UnionTerritoryTaxRates` (one row type, three set types), `GstReturnDueDates`, `QuarterlyReturnStates` and `GstReturnThresholds`. Contracts carry no helper that computes a due date or a tax: each rule belongs to and is tested by its consumer.
- **Generic reads in Contracts.** `IStatutoryParameterHistory` answers the sets with their version in force and next, a set's definition and versions with what each added, changed and removed, and the rows and changes of the version in force on a date. The viewer uses it, and it is the contract the Enterprise compliance assistant's digest builds on ([ADR-0030](0030-community-and-enterprise-editions.md)).
- **The rate entries stay faithful; the link to codes is a labelled reading.** Each rate set holds its notification's entries as the Table writes them (the entry's number, the heading codes column (2) names, column (3)'s words as printed, bracketed references included, without the notification's Explanations, the rate, the condition), each row citing its own tax's notification and clause, and no six-digit code; the first versions of the IGST and UTGST sets also cite the corrigenda of 30 June 2017 that tie their headings to the CGST Annexure. `gst.sac-codes` holds the Annexure verbatim. `gst.sac-rate-entries`, basis `interpretation` and shown as the ERP's reading, names for each six-digit code in scope and for heading 9983 the entry its services fall under by default and why, citing Explanation (ii) of 11/2017-CTR: 21(ii) for heading 9983 and its codes, 21(i) for 998363, 17(ii) for 997331. Entries that apply by what a supply is (21(ia), 38) are no code's default; the tax engine offers them.
- **The first datasets** are eight sets: `gst.sac-codes`, `gst.central-tax-rates`, `gst.integrated-tax-rates`, `gst.union-territory-tax-rates`, `gst.sac-rate-entries`, `gst.return-due-dates` (each rule's due day, months after the period's last month, IFF's opening day and the instrument's own words), `gst.quarterly-return-states` (each GST State code of rule 61(1)(ii)'s two lists, referencing its due-date rule) and `gst.return-thresholds`. Nothing the research could not confirm is seeded: 26 and 97 are in neither quarterly list, so the store answers no quarterly GSTR-3B date for them; the GSTR-9 exemption is absent in every year (owner decision), because only a newsletter carries 15/2025-CT, which grants it from 2024-25, and a set holding 2021-22 to 2023-24 would state either a lapse or a continuation no instrument read confirms; entry 39 and the Explanations are absent.
- **Two routes, one permission.** `GET /api/compliance/statutory-parameters/parameter-sets` (the sets, by title, each with its version in force today in India and the next) and `GET …/parameter-sets/{key}?on=` (the definition, every version with its change counts, and the version in force on the date, today when absent, with its rows and changes; 404 `parameter-set.not-found`), under `compliance.statutory-parameters.parameter-sets.read`, kept an hour by the browser (`WithReferenceDataCaching`). A seeded set is addressed by its stable key rather than a GUID id, the one exception to the routes' GUID ids, written in `backend/CLAUDE.md`; a key off its pattern is 400 `request.invalid` keyed `key`, and a malformed `on` fails binding with 400 `request.malformed`. Every set, whatever its shape, travels as columns plus cells of one `{ column, value }` shape, so the contract needs no union or dictionary. No route records, corrects or removes a parameter.
- **The pages.** "Statutory parameters" under "Compliance" lists the sets as cards (the version in force today and any upcoming one); a set's page shows its versions with their instruments, the entries of a chosen version (`?version=`) searched and paged on the web server, and what changed from the version before. The searched list of the reference-data pages moves to `shared/lists/searched/`, because a second module uses it, and takes its caller's address as data (a path and the parameters to keep, such as `version`) for its form, links and redirect, and, when a page composes several cards, the caller's navigation context.
- **Tests.** Unit tests of every column, date and coverage rule, the diff, the canonical cells and the typed reading, and a test holding every typed record to its file; integration tests that run the seeder twice and put back a changed or stray record; boundary tests that ask every row of every file on the day before it starts, its first and last day and the day after, with dates taken from the files; golden-value tests that pin each version of each dataset at its boundary dates beside its instrument; a seeder test that leaves nothing written when one of its files is defective; a host test that the version in force flips at midnight in India; endpoint, permission and feature-gate tests; Playwright on both pages.

## Options rejected

- **A table per dataset**: an entity, configuration, migration, seeder, endpoint, viewer and diff for each of about twenty sets, and a digest that would need a diff per table.
- **A JSON payload per version**: a change record would hold one opaque document per change and repeat every unchanged row, and nothing could page or look up a row in SQL.
- **Every row copied into every version**: a correction would have to be made in each copy; the dated rows give the same snapshot with one row per change.
- **Versions written as patches** (add, replace, remove): harder to read than dated rows and still a snapshot to compute.
- **Columns declared in C#**: the titles and kinds belong beside the data they describe, and the generic viewer would then need code for each set.
- **One rate set with CGST, IGST and UTGST columns**: each tax has its own instrument and clause, which one row could not cite.
- **The code-to-entry link inside the rate entries or the SAC codes**: it would mix the ERP's reading into a government table.
- **Due-date and tax helpers in Contracts**: logic with no owner; the calendar and tax engines own them.
- **A server cache**: nothing reads often yet, and a cache would hide a reseeded set until the API restarts.
- **A free date chooser on the page**: the versions are the dates at which anything changes.
- **An editor for parameters**: the owner keeps statutory changes in releases.
- **GSTN's `HSN_SAC.xlsx` as the source of SAC codes**: it is not a faithful copy of the Annexure.
- **Seeding inferences** (26 in the first quarterly list, 97, the GSTR-9 exemption from 2024-25 and so in any year): the store holds what an instrument says.
- **Pinning each version by a digest of its rows** instead of golden values: a failing digest would not show which value changed; the owner chose golden values beside their instruments.
- **A second copy of the GST State codes**: `Platform/ReferenceData` owns them.

## Consequences

- A new dataset is a seed file, a typed Contracts record and its tests, with no migration and no new route or page; a new kind or a composite key is a small change of the reader, the typed reading and the web's formatting.
- A notification becomes a release: its rows end and start in the file, its version is declared with its instruments, the migrator applies it, and the audit trail shows every changed row under the set; consumers keep the values they used, never a row's id.
- A set of many thousand rows would need paging in the API; today a version's rows are answered whole.
- The calendar engine asks for a quarterly GSTR-3B date when a registration's State is 26 or 97, and owns one-off extensions; the calendar engine also researches the GSTR-9 exemption of every year; the tax engine owns the date of supply, section 14, cess, exemptions, reverse charge and exports; `finance-core-company-profile` adds each State's SGST entries as a set keyed by State code.
- The reference-data pages read their searched list from `shared/lists/searched/`, whose test ids are `searched-list*`.
