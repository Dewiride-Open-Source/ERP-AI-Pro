# Platform / ReferenceData

The lists every part of the ERP shares, and the rupee value of foreign currencies by date ([ADR-0062](../../../../docs/adr/0062-reference-data-seeded-lists-every-iso-currency-dated-exchange-rates-and-financial-periods.md)): the countries, India's States and Union Territories with their GST State codes, the currencies and the April-to-March financial years with their quarters and monthly periods, which every employee reads, and the exchange rates, which the Finance role and administrators record by hand or from a file, correct and remove.

| Aspect | Value |
|---|---|
| Assembly | `Dewiride.Erp.Modules.Platform.ReferenceData` |
| Schema | `platform_reference_data` (`ReferenceDataDbContext`): `Countries` (the code, unique, and the English short name), `IndianStates` (a GST State code from a date: the name, the kind, the last day and the instrument that dates it; unique by code and start) and `ExchangeRates` (currency, day, rate, quotation unit and source; soft-deletable and versioned; one undeleted rate per currency and day); all three audited |
| Seeding | `CountrySeeder` (order 100) and `IndianStateSeeder` (order 110) make their tables equal to `countries.json` and `indian-states.json`, embedded in the assembly and read strictly: a missing row is added, a changed one follows the file and one the file no longer lists is deleted, each change recorded by the system; no person changes these rows |
| Currencies | no table: the 155 currencies of `Kernel.Monetary.Currency` (ISO 4217 List One of 2026-09-17), the ones a `Money` can hold |
| Route group | `/api/platform/reference-data`: `GET /countries`, `GET /indian-states` (`?on=`), `GET /currencies` and `GET /financial-years` (`?from=&to=`), whole lists kept by the browser for an hour (`WithReferenceDataCaching`); `GET /exchange-rates` (paged: `page`, `pageSize`, `sort` on `rateDate` and `currency`, the newest day first by default, `filter` on `currency` with `eq`, `ne` and `in` and on `rateDate` with comparisons), `GET /exchange-rates/{id}`, `POST /exchange-rates` and `POST /exchange-rates/imports` (with `Idempotency-Key`), `PUT /exchange-rates/{id}` (with the rate's `version`) and `DELETE /exchange-rates/{id}` |
| Feature flag | `Erp.Modules.Platform.ReferenceData`; while it is off the routes answer 404 `feature.disabled` and the pickers other modules show offer nothing |
| Permissions | `platform.reference-data.lists.read` (the four lists), held by Employee; `platform.reference-data.exchange-rates.read` (the two `GET` routes of the rates), `.record` (record and import) and `.correct` (correct and remove), held by Finance; Administrator holds all four (declared in `ReferenceDataPermissions`, [ADR-0041](../../../../docs/adr/0041-roles-and-permissions.md)); anyone else answers 403 `request.forbidden` |
| Rules | a rate is the rupees for 1, 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000 or 100,000 units of a currency other than INR, more than 0 and at most 1,000,000 with at most four decimal places, for today or an earlier day by India's time; a second rate of a currency and day answers 409 `exchange-rate.already-recorded`; an import of 1 to 1,000 rows records all of them or none, leaves a row equal in rate and unit to the recorded one as it is and refuses, keyed `rates[i].<field>`, a row that breaks a rule, repeats a currency and day of the file or differs from a recorded rate |
| Change records | every change is a row of `audit.ChangeRecords` under `platform.reference-data.countries`, `.indian-states` or `.exchange-rates` (`RecordChangesAs`), named by `CountryRecordNames` (the country), `IndianStateRecordNames` (the code and name) and `ExchangeRateRecordNames` (the currency and day, a removed rate included) in `Integration/` ([ADR-0043](../../../../docs/adr/0043-change-records-and-the-append-only-audit-schema.md)) |
| Contract | `ReferenceDataPermissions`; `ICountries`, `IIndianStates` (the State codes of a date) and `IExchangeRates` (`RateOnAsync`: the latest rate on or before a date), implemented in `PublicApi/` |
| Events | none |
