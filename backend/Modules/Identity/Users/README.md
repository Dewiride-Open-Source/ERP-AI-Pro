# Identity / Users

The people who use the ERP: one record per person, created at their first sign-in, registered beforehand by an administrator by work email or invited from the company directory in Microsoft Entra, and consulted at every sign-in and on every request of a session or a person's bearer token ([ADR-0039](../../../../docs/adr/0039-people-and-their-admission.md), [ADR-0040](../../../../docs/adr/0040-directory-invitations.md)), the roles each person holds in the ERP besides those their app roles give ([ADR-0041](../../../../docs/adr/0041-roles-and-permissions.md)), the organisation they work in: the department tree, the designations and each person's dated positions ([ADR-0042](../../../../docs/adr/0042-departments-designations-and-positions.md)), and the end of a person's access: the check of the directory's accounts, the handover of a deactivated person's team and the guard against two administrators removing each other ([ADR-0047](../../../../docs/adr/0047-user-lifecycle-the-account-check-and-the-team-handover.md)).

| Aspect | Value |
|---|---|
| Assembly | `Dewiride.Erp.Modules.Identity.Users` |
| Schema | `identity_users` (`UsersDbContext`): `Users` (the Entra object id, display name and work email Entra keeps, the employee code, phone number and date of joining administrators keep, the status and why the record was deactivated (`DeactivatedBecause`: 1 an administrator, 2 the account disabled, 3 the account deleted; null while active), the roles assigned in the ERP (`Roles`, a `json` array of `Finance`, `Hr` and `Manager`) and the last sign-in time; soft-deletable, versioned, audited), `PersonThemes` (the colour theme a person chose, keyed by their record's id: `Theme` 1 system, 2 light, 3 dark, and `ChoiceId`, which changes with the theme; audited, never versioned, so a choice moves none of the record's versions; no row until the person first chooses a theme other than system), `Departments` (name, parent; soft-deletable, versioned, audited), `Designations` (name; soft-deletable, versioned, audited) and `Positions` (a person's department, designation and manager from a date until their next; audited, never edited) |
| Route group | `/api/identity/users`, each route requiring its permission: the people routes, which administrators alone hold: `GET /` (paged, sorted, filtered) and `GET /{id}` (`read`), `POST /` (by work email, with `Idempotency-Key`), `GET /directory?search=` (the company directory, read through `IPeopleDirectory` on the administrator's behalf) and `POST /invitations` (a directory person by Entra object id, with `Idempotency-Key`) (`register`; roles named in either also need `assign-roles`), `PUT /{id}` (`update`), `PUT /{id}/status` (`change-status`; a deactivation of a person with a team names in `team` who the team reports to from today, which also needs `identity.users.positions.record` and `.withdraw`) and `PUT /{id}/roles` (`assign-roles`), each with the record's `version`, `DELETE /{id}` (`delete`), `POST /directory/account-check` (`change-status`: the Entra accounts of the active linked records, read through `IDirectoryAccounts` with the ERP's own permission, and the deactivation of each disabled or deleted one); `/departments` and `/designations` (list and get, which every employee reads; create with `Idempotency-Key`, update with the `version`, delete); `/{id}/positions` (list, get, record with `Idempotency-Key`, withdraw); `GET /{id}/team` (`identity.users.positions.read`: the people whose position names the person as manager today or from a later date, by display name); `GET /org-chart?on=` (today's for every employee, another date's for administrators); `GET /permissions` (`read`: every permission the modules declare, with what it allows and the built-in roles that hold it, from the `PermissionCatalog` the host built at startup, [ADR-0045](../../../../docs/adr/0045-people-pages-and-the-permission-catalogue.md)); and the caller's own record, which any signed-in person may use without a permission: `GET /me` (their details, today's position, the built-in roles they hold, through `ICallerRoles`, and their theme), `GET /me/photo` (their photo in the company directory, read through `IPeopleDirectory` on their behalf, 204 when it holds none) and `PUT /me/theme` (the colour theme they chose, the latest choice winning) |
| Feature flag | `Erp.Modules.Identity.Users` (the routes only; admission runs whatever the flag says) |
| Contracts | `UsersPermissions` (`identity.users.people.read`, `.register`, `.update`, `.change-status`, `.delete`, `.assign-roles`; `identity.users.departments.*` and `identity.users.designations.*` with `read`, `create`, `update`, `delete`; `identity.users.positions.read`, `.record`, `.withdraw`; `identity.users.org-chart.read`; the reads of departments, designations and the chart declared for Employee, the rest for Administrator alone), `PersonStatus`, `PersonDeactivationReason` (`administrator`, `accountDisabled`, `accountDeleted`: why a record was deactivated, `deactivatedBecause` on the wire), `PersonRole` (`finance`, `hr`, `manager`: the roles the ERP assigns), `ThemePreference` (`system`, `light`, `dark`: the colour theme a person chose, on the wire of `GET /me` and `PUT /me/theme`) |
| Integration | `UserAdmission` implements `IPersonAdmission` of `BuildingBlocks.Application`: `AdmitAsync` creates or refreshes the person's record at every sign-in and refuses a deactivated one, `AdmissionOfAsync` answers the roles assigned to the active record that names the Entra object id, or nothing when no such record admits the person |
| Directory | `IPeopleDirectory` of `BuildingBlocks.Application` (`Actors/PeopleDirectory`), implemented by `BuildingBlocks.Authentication` over Microsoft Graph; the endpoints read it and hand the people to `FindRegisteredPeopleQuery` and `InvitePersonCommand`, so no handler needs a signed-in person, and `GET /me/photo` answers the caller's own photo from it directly. `IDirectoryAccounts` of the same folder answers whether accounts are enabled, disabled or deleted, read with the application permission `User.Read.All`; `DirectoryAccountCheck` takes it as an argument, between `ListActiveAccountsQuery` and `ApplyAccountStatesCommand`, so any host that composes the module can run the check with the directory its caller holds. An answer that holds no enabled account changes nothing and answers 503 `directory.unavailable`; a person the check deactivates keeps their team, whose records then answer `managerActive` false |
| Organisation writes | every write to the department tree or the positions, every status change and every delete of a person, department or designation, and the account check, take the lock `identity_users.organisation` of its transaction (`OrganisationLock`), so the cycle and in-use rules are checked one write after another; a status change, a deletion of a person and the account check then read the records of the person making them (`ActingPerson`) and are refused with 409 `user.actor-inactive` when every one is deactivated or deleted, so two administrators removing each other at once leave one of them. A person's team is everyone whose position names them as manager and holds today or later (`TeamPositions`): deactivating a person with a team needs who the team reports to from today (422 `user.team-handover-required`; the new manager active, not the person, not in the team, closing no reporting cycle: `user.team-manager-not-found`, `-inactive`, `-same-person`, `-in-team`, `user.team-reporting-cycle`), and the team's positions move in the deactivation's transaction (`TeamHandoverPlan`); deleting a person with a team is refused (409 `user.manages-people`) |
| Change records | every change to a person's record (their details, status, the reason they were deactivated and roles), a department, a designation, a position or a person's colour theme is a row of `audit.ChangeRecords`, written by the persistence building block in the same save ([ADR-0043](../../../../docs/adr/0043-change-records-and-the-append-only-audit-schema.md)), under the keys `identity.users.people`, `identity.users.departments`, `identity.users.designations`, `identity.users.positions` and `identity.users.themes` (`RecordChangesAs` in each configuration); a position's changes are filed under its person (`FileChangesUnder(p => p.UserId)`), and so are a theme's (`FileChangesUnder(t => t.Id)`), so a person's history shows their moves, and a withdrawn position, including one a team handover replaces, is `Deleted` with its last values; `User.LastSignedInAt` and `PersonTheme.ChoiceId` are left out (`ExcludeFromChangeRecords`), so a sign-in that changes nothing else writes no record, nor does saving the theme a person holds already, while a person's first sign-in records the creation of their record with them as its actor |
| Names for the history | `UserRecordNames` (`Integration/`) implements `IRecordNames` of `BuildingBlocks.Persistence` for people (their display name), departments and designations (their name), deleted ones included, and `IPersonNames` of `BuildingBlocks.Application` for actors (for an object id that two records name, the record not deleted before the newer one), so `Platform/Audit` names them without referencing this module |
| Events | none |
